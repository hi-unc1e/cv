## What I Do

I'm @Unc1e. I work in network security, and I also write about engineering practice, work, and life.

This site keeps the articles I've published publicly and the projects I've built. Yuque is my pocket notebook; this site is a portable, long-lasting copy.

## Writing Principles

> Steven N.S. Cheung: In any discipline, I value the fundamentals.

> Li Xiaolai: Coding may seem to have a high barrier at first, but that's an illusion — plenty of things are far harder to get into. What's worth writing in code is creation, or at least an effective solution to a meaningful problem; what's worth writing in words is thought. In the end, it's just the most basic tool — thinking ability is what counts.

> Herbert A. Simon: "Best" is the enemy of good.
